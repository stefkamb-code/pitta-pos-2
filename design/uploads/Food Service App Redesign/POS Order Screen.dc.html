BRIEFΠίττα του Παππού — POS remake · order-taking screen

The screen employees stare at all day is the product-selection screen, so that's where the direction matters most — I'm exploring it first. All three keep the same bones as a familiar POS (order-type at top, product list in the middle, running ticket on the right) so staff aren't relearning anything, and all three wear the Modernist look: flat, red-on-white, 2 px rules, no rounded corners, flush-left labels, Archivo. Bilingual EL/EN toggle top-right; no product photos (text + price, faster to scan). They differ only in how you add a product fast. Pick one and I'll build the full flow — order type → customer lookup → products → delivery type → print — in that direction.

2Product customization — pick a pita, choose ingredients right away

Tapping a product opens its ingredients immediately, like your current screen but readable: included ingredients start on and you tap to drop them ("χωρίς κρεμμύδι"), extras add with one tap and step up to double (Διπλό / ×2), plus bread type (Ελληνική / Αραβική / Ψωμί), Σκέτο, per-item note, discount and Χωρίς Χρέωση. Same real ingredient list you already use. Two placements below — build either onto whichever product screen you pick from turn 1.
